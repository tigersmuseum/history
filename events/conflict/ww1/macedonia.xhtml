Macedonian front

This campaign starts with the fall of Serbia in late 1915, and ends with the Allied offensive in September 1918.

Also known as the Salonica front

Battle of Krivolak

21st - 22nd October 1915

Battle of Kosturino

6th - 12th December 1915

The battle honour is Kosturino.

First Battle of Doiran

9th - 18th August 1916

Battle of Florina

17th - 28th August 1916

Also known as the Battle of Lerin or Chegan offensive

Monastir offensive

12th September - 11th December 1916

Battle of Kaymakchalan

12th - 30th September 1916

Also known as the Battle of Kajmakcalan

Battle of Malka Nidzhe

12th - 14th September 1916

Also known as the Battle of Gornichevo

Operations in the Struma Valley

30th September - 31st October 1916

The battle honour is Struma.

Battle of the Crna Bend

14th - 19th October 1916

Also known as the Battle of the River Cherna

Second Battle of Doiran

22nd April - 9th May 1917

The battle honour is Doiran 1917.

Battle of Skra-di-Legen

29th - 30th May 1918

Also known as the Battle of Skora di Legen

Vardar offensive

15th - 29th September 1918

Battle of Dobro Pole

15th - 18th September 1918

Third Battle of Doiran

18th - 19th September 1918

The battle honour is Doiran 1918.
